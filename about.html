About Us

Our Mission

Health literacy is essential for making good decisions, but many teens don’t receive the tools they need. We aim to close this gap by offering clear, culturally sensitive, teen-friendly explanations of common medical topics.

Why This Matters

  • Many teens avoid care because they don’t know what to expect.
  • Medical terms can feel intimidating.
  • Immigrant, multilingual, or low-income teens especially face barriers.
  • Better health literacy leads to safer choices and earlier medical care.

Who We Serve

  • Pennsylvania teens
  • New immigrant youth
  • Students navigating healthcare independently
  • Anyone wanting reliable, simple health information

Our Story

“I started this project after seeing how many teens, especially from immigrant families, struggle with understanding doctor visits, medications, and when to seek care. I wanted to create a free resource that explains everything clearly, without judgment, so teens can feel safe and informed.”
