Mental Health Basics

Common Signs of Stress/Anxiety

  • Trouble sleeping
  • Loss of appetite
  • Feeling overwhelmed
  • Heart racing

Depression Basics

  • Persistent sadness
  • Loss of interest
  • Feeling hopeless
  • Low energy

How to Ask for Help

  • “I’ve been feeling really overwhelmed. Can we talk?”
  • “I don’t feel like myself lately.”

Who to Talk To

  • School counselor
  • Trusted adult
  • Doctor
  • Crisis hotline (988)
