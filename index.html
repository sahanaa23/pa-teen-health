Welcome!

Understanding health shouldn't be confusing. The Pennsylvania Teen Health Literacy Project provides simple, teen-friendly guides to help you navigate doctor visits, pharmacies, mental health, reproductive health, and more. Our goal is to help every teen feel confident advocating for themselves.

Who This Guide Is For

Teens and young adults, first-time patients, immigrants, first-generation families, or anyone feeling unsure about medical visits.

Purpose of This Guide

Provides educational information and sample scripts for communicating with healthcare providers. Not a substitute for medical advice or emergency care.

Privacy & Confidentiality

“Is this visit confidential?”

“Will my guardian(s) be notified?”

“Who can access my records?”

Emergency Notice

This guide is for educational purposes only.
If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room immediately.

Accessibility & Language Access

Healthcare should be accessible to everyone. You have the right to:

  • Request an interpreter
  • Ask for written instructions
  • Ask providers to speak more slowly or clearly
  • Request accommodations for disabilities
