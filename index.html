Home

Understanding health shouldn't be confusing. The Pennsylvania Teen Health Literacy Project provides simple, accessible guides to help teens navigate doctor visits, pharmacies, mental health, reproductive health, and basic medical decisions. Our goal is to help every teen feel confident advocating for themselves and accessing care.

Who This Guide Is For

This guide is designed for teens and young adults who want to better understand how to access healthcare. It is especially helpful for first-time patients, immigrants, first-generation families, and anyone who feels unsure or nervous about medical visits.

Purpose of This Guide

This guide provides general educational information and sample scripts to help users communicate with healthcare providers. It is not a substitute for medical advice or emergency care.

Privacy & Confidentiality

Many teens worry about who can see their medical information. Privacy rules depend on age, state laws, and the type of care. You are allowed to ask:

  • “Is this visit confidential?”
  • “Will my parent or guardian be notified?”
  • “Who can access my records?”
Emergency Notice

This guide is for educational purposes only.
If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room immediately.

Accessibility & Language Access

Healthcare should be accessible to everyone. You have the right to:

  • Request an interpreter
  • Ask for written instructions
  • Ask providers to speak more slowly or clearly
  • Request accommodations for disabilities
