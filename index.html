Understanding health shouldn't be confusing. The Pennsylvania Teen Health Literacy Project provides simple, accessible guides to help teens navigate doctor visits, pharmacies, mental health, reproductive health, and basic medical decisions. Our goal is to help every teen feel confident advocating for themselves and accessing care.
