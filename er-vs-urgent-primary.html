ER vs. Urgent Care vs. Primary Care

Emergency Room (ER)

Go for issues which may be life threatening or dangerous:

  • Trouble breathing
  • Severe bleeding
  • Chest pain
  • Broken bones with deformity

Emergency Scripts

Calling 911

“I need an ambulance. I’m at ___. The problem is ___.”

At the ER

“I’m having ___ symptoms and they started ___ ago.”

Urgent Care

Good for same-day but not life-threatening:

  • Flu symptoms
  • Minor injuries
  • Pink eye
  • Rashes
  • Ear infections

Primary Care Doctor

Good for:

  • Regular checkups
  • Vaccines
  • Long-term concerns
  • Birth control questions
  • Mental health referrals

Cost Differences (general)

  • Primary care: lowest
  • Urgent care: medium
  • ER: most expensive
Emergency Notice

This guide is for educational purposes only.
If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room immediately.
