ER vs. Urgent Care vs. Primary Care

Emergency Room (ER)

Go for issues which may be life threatening or dangerous:

  • Trouble breathing
  • Severe bleeding
  • Chest pain
  • Broken bones with deformity

Urgent Care

Good for same-day but not life-threatening:

  • Flu symptoms
  • Minor injuries
  • Pink eye
  • Rashes
  • Ear infections

Primary Care Doctor

Good for:

  • Regular checkups
  • Vaccines
  • Long-term concerns
  • Birth control questions
  • Mental health referrals

Cost Differences (general)

  • Primary care: lowest
  • Urgent care: medium
  • ER: most expensive
