Period & Reproductive Health

What’s Normal

  • Cycles 21–35 days
  • Cramping
  • Light to moderate bleeding
  • Mood swings

When to See a Doctor

  • Very heavy bleeding
  • Severe pain
  • No period for 3+ months
  • Symptoms that interfere with daily life

Menstrual Hygiene Tips

  • Change pads/tampons every 3–4 hours (more if heavy bleeding)
  • Pain relief like ibuprofen
  • Track your cycle with an app (Flo)
Emergency Notice

This guide is for educational purposes only.
If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room immediately.

Accessibility & Language Access

Healthcare should be accessible to everyone. You have the right to:

  • Request an interpreter
  • Ask for written instructions
  • Ask providers to speak more slowly or clearly
  • Request accommodations for disabilities
