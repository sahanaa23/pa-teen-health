Pharmacy Basics

Refills

A refill is when you get more of a medication you already have a prescription for.

Over-the-Counter (OTC) Medications

You can buy these without a prescription (ibuprofen, allergy meds, etc.)

How to Read a Medication Label

  • Active ingredient
  • Dosage
  • How often to take it
  • Warnings

Examples

  • “Take 1 tablet every 6 hours”
  • “Do not take with alcohol”
