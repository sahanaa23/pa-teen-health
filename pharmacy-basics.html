Pharmacy Basics

Refills

A refill is when you get more of a medication you already have a prescription for.

Over-the-Counter (OTC) Medications

You can buy these without a prescription (ibuprofen, allergy meds, etc.).

How to Read a Medication Label

  • Active ingredient
  • Dosage
  • How often to take it
  • Warnings

Examples

  • “Take 1 tablet every 6 hours”
  • “Do not take with alcohol”

Picking Up a Prescription

When you arrive at the pharmacy, you can say:

“Hi, I’m here to pick up a prescription for ___.”

Questions You Are Allowed to Ask

  • What is this medication for?
  • How and when do I take it?
  • Are there side effects?
  • Can I take it with other medications?
  • How much does it cost?

If You Can’t Afford Medication

You can ask the pharmacist:

“Is there a generic version or a discount available?”

If You Miss a Dose

Ask the pharmacist or read the medication label — do not guess.
