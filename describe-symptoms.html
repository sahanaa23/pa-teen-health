How to Describe Symptoms

Simple Symptom Phrases

  • “The pain is sharp / dull / burning.”
  • “It started ___ days ago.”
  • “It’s getting better / worse.”
  • “It hurts when I ___.”

Pain Scale (0–10)

0 = no pain
10 = worst pain of your life

Examples

  • “I have a sore throat and a fever of 101°F.”
  • “My chest feels tight when I breathe.”
  • “I feel dizzy when I stand up.”
