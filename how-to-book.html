How to Book a Doctor’s Appointment

What You Need First

  • Name
  • Date of birth
  • Reason for visit
  • Insurance info (if you have it)
  • Preferred date/time

Phone Script

“Hi, my name is ___. I’m calling to schedule a doctor’s appointment. I’m coming in for ___ (reason). Do you have availability this week or next week?”

If you don’t have insurance

  • Many clinics accept patients without insurance
  • Ask: “Do you offer sliding scale or low-cost visits?”

What They Might Ask You

  • Symptoms
  • Preferred doctor
  • Any medications you take
  • Your phone number
  • What to bring:
    • ID (school ID is okay)
    • Insurance card
    • Medication list
    • Notes about symptoms
